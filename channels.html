    How rooms work, in full

    Every coin launched on cutroom opens a room: a clipping channel for the streamers it picks. The room watches their streams live, hears every word, reads chat, finds the moments, has an editor pass decide what is worth posting, and cuts the clips in about a minute. Before launch, the rooms here are test rooms: the same room working real streams, so you can judge its taste before any coin exists.

    A room only clips streamers with an active, verified clipping program in the library, follows each program's rules, and credits every clip. Views are submitted to the programs; payouts cover the room's running costs and the profit buys back its coin. Every payout and buyback is listed on the room's page.
