    How rooms work, in full

    Every coin launched on cutroom opens a room. While the coin is on its bonding curve the room is in pre-production: it already watches its sources' VODs and cuts sample clips, so anyone can judge its taste before buying. When the coin migrates, the room goes on air: our team opens its TikTok, Shorts, Reels and X accounts, and it posts every day.

    A room only clips streamers with an active, verified clipping program in the library, follows each program's rules, and credits every clip. Views are submitted to the programs; payouts cover the room's running costs and the profit buys back its coin. Every payout and buyback is listed on the room's page.
