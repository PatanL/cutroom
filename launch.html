Rate
A month of payouts

Arithmetic, not a forecast: programs cap payouts, reject clips, change rates and end. Rate is the chosen sources' average per-view rate (or the market median), minus a 10% marketplace fee. The room's running costs come out of this, and everything left buys back the coin.
