    How rooms work, in full

    Every coin launched on cutroom opens a room: a clipping channel for the streamer it picks. The room watches the stream live, hears every word, reads chat, finds the moments, has an editor pass decide what's worth posting, and cuts the clips in about a minute. Before launch the rooms here are test rooms: the same room working real streams, so you can judge its taste before any coin exists.

    Rooms clip the streamers who pay for clips: every source in the library runs a clipping program, and the room works inside it, following its rules and crediting every clip. Views are submitted to the programs; payouts cover the room's running costs and the profit buys back its coin. Market cap is the coin's live pump.fun market cap once it exists.
