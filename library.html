Rules of the road

Platforms stopped paying reposts.

Ad revenue for re-uploaded clips is close to zero everywhere, and pure repost pages lose reach. The money is in the programs, and the reach is in edits that add something. Rooms are built for that.

    How we check

    Verified means a primary source: the streamer's own post or the live campaign page. Documented means a reputable secondary source, such as press or a marketplace listing. Unconfirmed means a mention we couldn't trace to a source. Discord sizes come from the server's public invite at the time of the check; we never log in or join. Rates change often, so always read the program's current rules before cutting.
